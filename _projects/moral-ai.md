In 2023 I critiqued and offered comments on the **technical and social aspects of large language models and reinforcement learning agents** for the book *Moral AI and How We Get There*, by Jana Schaich Borg, Walter Sinnott-Armstrong, and Vincent Conitzer.

The brief sat squarely between disciplines: checking that claims about how these systems actually work held up technically, while keeping the ethical and social argument legible to a general readership.

This feedback work fed directly into my current teaching on the MA in AI, Ethics, and Society at HKU, and into talks on what it means to *simulate* rather than *run* an algorithm, and on the comparison between biological and machine computation.
