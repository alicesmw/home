A methods course on the taught-postgraduate MA in **AI, Ethics, and Society** at HKU, co-taught with tutorial sections.

Students work on the formal tools needed to state claims about AI systems precisely enough to evaluate them — and on recognising where a formalisation has quietly smuggled in a normative assumption.
