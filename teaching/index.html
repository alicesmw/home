teaching

Courses, mentorship, and guest lectures.

I currently teach on two taught-postgraduate MA programmes at the University of Hong Kong — AI, Ethics, and Society and Philosophy, Politics, and Economics — co-teaching three courses and leading two tutorial sections.

2025

Fundamentals of AI, Data, and Algorithms (PHIL7001)

MA in AI, Ethics, and Society Alice Wong (co-taught)

How contemporary AI systems actually work — data, learning algorithms, and the assumptions built into them — for students arriving from philosophy and the social sciences.

Formal Methods for AI, Ethics, and Society (PHIL7010)

MA in AI, Ethics, and Society Alice Wong (co-taught)

Logic, formal reasoning, and quantitative methods applied to questions about AI systems and their governance.

Foundations of PPE: Reasons and Methods

MA in Philosophy, Politics, and Economics Alice Wong (co-taught)

The methodological toolkit shared across philosophy, politics, and economics, and what each discipline means by a good reason.
